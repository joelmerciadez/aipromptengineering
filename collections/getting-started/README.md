## How to use

1. Open a linked prompt.
2. Copy the body into your model of choice.
3. Fill every `{{placeholder}}`.
4. Adjust constraints to match your project.

## Add another collection

Create `collections/<collection-name>/README.md` with a short intro and a table of links. Prefer linking to prompt files instead of copying their full text.
